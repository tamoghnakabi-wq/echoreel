First release of Echoreel.

- Watches your YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts and relays each new video to the others.
- Post a video once from the app and send it everywhere.
- Spots its own relays and re-uploads, so nothing loops or posts twice.
- Fits landscape videos to 9:16 for Reels and TikTok.
- Ask-first mode: check captions before anything is posted.
- Updates itself: new versions download in the background and install when Echoreel is idle.
