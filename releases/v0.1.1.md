Fixes from a full check of the app.

- A video you quit Echoreel in the middle of uploading now carries on after a restart, and YouTube picks up where it stopped instead of posting it twice.
- "Relay now" with every destination switched off no longer leaves the video waiting forever. Videos stuck that way are cleaned up on start.
- With "Only relay YouTube Shorts" on, long YouTube videos are skipped without downloading them first.
- Posting a large file from the app no longer freezes the window while it's copied.
- Camera files with ProRes video or uncompressed audio are converted for YouTube instead of failing.
- After an interrupted YouTube download, Echoreel no longer picks up a half-finished leftover file.
- The sign-in page always appears in your browser, can't be fed outside text, and a stray link can't cancel your sign-in.
- The window keeps up with fast changes, and opening Echoreel from a notification takes you to that video.
- "Relay anyway" buttons are no longer cut off in a narrow window.
- Mac: when it starts at login, Echoreel stays in the menu bar instead of opening its window.
- Windows: the "Start Echoreel when I log in" switch shows its real setting.
