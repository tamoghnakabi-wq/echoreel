- Echoreel has its own menu bar on macOS. The Echoreel menu has Check for Updates…, shows an update's download progress, and offers Restart to Update once it's ready.
- New keyboard shortcuts: ⌘N posts a video, ⌘1–⌘5 switch pages, ⌘R checks for new videos, ⇧⌘P pauses or resumes relaying, and ⌘, opens Settings.
- A Relay menu shows which accounts are being watched, and a Help menu links to the website, release notes, issue reporting and the log file.
- The menu bar icon's menu can check for updates too.
- On Windows, press Alt for the same menus. Check for Updates and About are at the end of Help.
- Reload and Developer Tools are no longer in the menus.
